# Discover Crew Technologies — Content & Copywriting Guidelines

This document outlines the required voice, tone, and content standards for Discover Crew Technologies.

---

## 1. Core Messaging Rule
Every sentence on the Discover Crew Technologies website must communicate at least one of:
- What Discover Crew does
- Who it helps
- What problem it solves
- What software it builds
- How its engineering process works
- What technologies it uses
- What proprietary products it offers (Ventura AI, Event OS)
- Why a business customer should contact the company

---

## 2. Forbidden Phrases & Buzzwords
DO NOT use futuristic filler, exaggerated claims, or artificial buzzwords:
- ❌ "We Build Tomorrow's Digital Reality"
- ❌ "Architects of silent protocols powering the next century of digital evolution"
- ❌ "Revolutionary / Cutting-edge / Next generation" (unless empirically justified)
- ❌ "Transform the future / Unlock limitless potential / Seamless innovation"
- ❌ "Game-changing / World-class / Industry-leading"

---

## 3. Approved Tone & Voice
- **Tone**: Professional, clear, concise, direct, human business English.
- **Perspective**: Written from the standpoint of experienced software engineering founders.
- **Structure**: Short sentences, active voice, explicit business benefits, clear action-oriented CTAs (`Start a Project`, `Explore Our Products`, `Discuss Your Software Project`).

---

## 4. On-Page SEO Requirements for New Pages
1. **Single H1**: Exactly one `<h1>` per page reflecting the primary topic.
2. **Metadata**: Unique `<title>` (50–60 chars) and `<meta name="description">` (140–160 chars).
3. **Canonical Header**: Always include canonical URL point to standard domain `https://discovercrewtechnologies.com/`.
4. **Structured JSON-LD Data**: Embed relevant `Organization`, `Service`, or `SoftwareApplication` schema.
5. **Internal Linking**: Link back to relevant service, product, and contact routes using natural anchor text.
